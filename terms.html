These terms apply when you register for a ONE in Marriage program through this website. By completing registration and payment, you agree to them.

What you are registering for

The Weekend Marriage Experience is a facilitated group program for couples, held 5–7 November 2026 in Sandy, Utah. The price is $3,000 per couple, not per person. Registration covers both partners.

Both partners must attend

This program is designed for two committed partners attending together, start to finish. The work happens between the two of you. If one partner cannot attend the full weekend, the program will not serve you as intended and we ask that you not register.

Screening

Every couple answers a short set of questions before registering. This is how we make sure the program is a good and safe fit. We reserve the right to decline or cancel a registration where the program is not appropriate, and in that case we will refund payment in full.

This is not therapy or crisis care

ONE in Marriage is an educational and facilitated experience. It is not therapy, medical care, psychiatric treatment, or a substitute for any of them, and no facilitator-client or therapist-client relationship is created. It is not designed or staffed to help where there is abuse, coercion, or danger in a relationship. If that is your situation, please contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 or call 911 in an emergency.

Payment

Payment is taken at registration through Stripe. We never see or store your card or bank details. All payments are final — see our refund policy.

Capacity and changes

Each weekend is limited to 25 couples and registration closes when it fills. We may need to change the venue, schedule or facilitators; if we cancel outright, you may choose a full refund or a place at a future weekend.

Your participation

You take part voluntarily and remain responsible for your own choices, wellbeing and conduct during the program. We ask every participant to keep what other couples share confidential.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, our total liability arising from your participation is limited to the amount you paid. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, including outcomes in your relationship.
