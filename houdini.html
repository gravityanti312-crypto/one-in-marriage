How these numbers are calculated

100 × selected lens points ÷ all assigned lens points. Assigned points include every answer option in every question, including choices you did not select.

The eight scores are independent indices and are not constrained to add to 100. Where competing answers carry points for the same lens, its highest attainable signal score may be below 100. No attainable-ceiling adjustment is applied.

Rankings use unrounded values. Displays are rounded to one decimal when needed. Exact ties share a rank.
