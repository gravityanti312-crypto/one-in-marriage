Registration & Payment

Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

The Weekend Marriage Experience is a small-group program limited to 25 couples. Because places are reserved for you specifically and preparation begins as soon as you register, all payments are final.

All registrations are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled. This applies from the moment payment is completed.

Why the policy is firm

Each weekend is capped at 25 couples so that every couple can be seen and heard. When you register, that place is held for you and closed to everyone else. Materials, facilitation and venue commitments are arranged around the confirmed group.

If you cannot attend

We understand that life happens. If something prevents you from attending, please contact us as early as you can at jacques@sharingvitality.com. While payments are not refundable, we will do what we reasonably can to help — that may include transferring your place to a future weekend, at our discretion. This is a courtesy, not an entitlement.

If we cancel

If ONE in Marriage cancels or reschedules a weekend for any reason, you may choose either a full refund of what you paid or a place at a future weekend.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you were charged twice or charged in error, contact us and we will refund the incorrect charge in full.

Questions before you pay

If anything about the program, the dates or this policy is unclear, please ask before registering rather than after. We would rather answer ten questions than take a registration you regret.
